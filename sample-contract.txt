5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. All deliverables are a work made for hire and Provider assigns all right, title and interest to Client.

6. INDEMNIFICATION. Provider shall indemnify Client against any claims arising from the services.

7. LIABILITY. In no event shall either party's liability exceed the fees paid in the prior 3 months.

8. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

9. NON-COMPETE. Provider shall not compete with Client within the same market for 12 months after termination.
